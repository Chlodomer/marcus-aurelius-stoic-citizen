## Method

1. Candidate wording was located through the public-domain Wikisource transcription.
2. Relevant pages of `Marcus Aurelius.pdf` were rendered as high-resolution images.
3. OCR was used as a checking aid, then decisive wording was read against the page images.
4. The application preserves Haines’s vocabulary and citation divisions while omitting footnote markers from excerpts when the footnote itself is not reproduced.
5. Every keyboard-accessible quotation is an exact, focused substring of the verified passage shown in the reader.

## Evidence and inference

- Haines’s note linking X.35 to anxiety about Commodus is labeled as an editorial interpretation, not as Marcus’s statement of motive.
- The Question 8 succession summary is labeled historical context. The application states that the *Meditations* does not directly explain Marcus’s succession decision.
